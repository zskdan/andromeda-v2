# Andromeda-v2 Concept of Operations (CONOPS)

## Project Definition

**Andromeda-v2** is a **120 mm diameter experimental sounding rocket and technology demonstrator**, designed for a nominal altitude of approximately **3 km**, with a design and operational envelope extending up to **10 km**.

Its primary purpose is not only to reach altitude, but to provide a **reusable flight-test platform for advanced onboard avionics, communication, navigation, and embedded-processing technologies** under real rocket flight conditions.

The vehicle will demonstrate a robust **narrowband (NB) telemetry and command/control link**, together with a **wideband (WB) communication capability for real-time transmission of video from multiple onboard cameras**. It will also integrate onboard navigation and state estimation, local telemetry and video recording, vehicle recovery, and a ground segment for command, reception, visualization, and data recording.

Andromeda-v2 is intended to be a **modular and evolvable technology-demonstration platform**. Communication waveforms, RF hardware, antennas, sensors, FPGA logic, onboard processing, flight software, and navigation algorithms can therefore be progressively developed, replaced, and validated through successive ground tests and flight campaigns.

The rocket itself is consequently **both the flight vehicle and the experimental platform**, allowing technologies to be validated under representative acceleration, vibration, rotation, RF propagation, and dynamic flight conditions.

## Technology-Demonstration Objectives

The following objectives form the draft v0.1 mission-success baseline. Values marked **TBD** remain open until supported by analysis, simulation, applicable launch constraints, or test evidence. Unless a row states otherwise, the flight evaluation window extends from detected liftoff through detected touchdown, and altitude is measured above the launch-site ground level (AGL).

For commanded video operation, planned intervals during which a stream or recorder is commanded off are logged but excluded from that channel's availability denominator. This exclusion does not apply to uncommanded outages.

The individual objectives are organized under nine umbrellas for planning and reporting. An umbrella may be reported as fully or partially achieved, but every child objective retains its own identifier, evidence, and verification result.
